British Army C-sUAS Small Arms & Shotgun Engagement Doctrine
1 Point-of-Aim Ambush
Do NOT attempt to fluidly track an 80+ mph FPV drone. Pick a spatial reference along its flight path, hold fixed lead ahead, and fire rapid bursts as it enters the beaten zone.
2 Section Volume Box
Single-rifle hit probability is under 5%. Section Commanders must issue Fire Control Orders creating a 5m × 5m airspace box ahead of incoming targets for synchronized rapid fire.
3 Shotgun Envelope (<50m)
L128A1 12-Gauge is highly effective under 50m for trench point defence. Shot spread (~0.75m–1.5m) forgives lead errors. Beyond 50m pellet pattern density degrades rapidly.
4 Shotgun Swing-Through
Unlike rifle ambush holds, 12-gauge engagements under 50m use a swing-through technique: insert sight behind target, sweep forward past the nose, pull trigger on lead point while continuing swing.
